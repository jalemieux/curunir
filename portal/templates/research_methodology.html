THE FRAMING

Catalyst → instruments → thesis.

Sell-side research almost always runs the other direction. A name lands on the desk, the analyst back-fills a thesis, and the catalysts come last (sometimes never). The result is a clean-looking document anchored to whichever ticker the firm wanted covered, not to whatever is actually moving the world.

This pipeline inverts it. The agent starts from a catalyst — a Polymarket probability moving, a podcast mention spiking, a Reddit thread converging on a name, an earnings print, a regulatory clock — and works forward to the instruments that express it and the thesis that holds them together.

PIPELINE
instrument→thesis→catalysts (maybe)

You already picked the ticker. You write a thesis. You list catalysts that would help. The ticker was load-bearing; everything else decorates.

PIPELINE
catalyst→instruments→thesis

You start with something that is actually moving. The instruments fall out of what expresses it cleanest. The thesis is the connective tissue, not the premise.

THE FLOW

What's under the hood.

A peer of the investment-memo skill — shares the same primitives, adds two forward-looking layers.
SHARED PRIMITIVES
deep-research
Open-ended web research across filings, news, transcripts, sell-side notes, and primary sources. The agent decomposes the question, runs parallel searches, and synthesizes findings with inline citations.
financial-analysis
Numbers, not narrative. Pulls statements, builds the relevant tables (DCF, sensitivities, peer comps, sum-of-the-parts), and stress-tests the assumptions that the thesis hinges on.
fact-checker
Every claim in the memo gets verified against at least one primary source before it ships. Unverifiable claims are either re-sourced, softened, or cut.
reddit
Operator and retail signal. Surfaces what people closest to the product or the trade are actually saying, including the disagreements. Catches stories before they reach the tape.
xai
Real-time X corpus access. Live discussion, breaking-news graph, and sentiment around the catalyst and the cap table.
FORWARD-LOOKING LAYERS
polymarket
Implied probabilities on the catalysts. Whatever the thesis is leaning on — a Fed move, an FDA decision, an election, an earnings beat — Polymarket has a price for it. The memo uses those prices to bracket scenarios instead of guessing.
podcast-corpus grep
Pressure-tests the narrative against what operators, founders, and investors are saying in long-form. Searches a transcript corpus for recent mentions of the company, sector, or catalyst — so the agent isn't just rebutting the obvious headlines.
