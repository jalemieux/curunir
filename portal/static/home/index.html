How it works

The models are in the cloud. Your data is not.

The model travels. Your information doesn't.

When you ask it something, Curunir sends the question to cloud-based language models and brings the answer back. Your portfolio, your notes, your conversations, your health research stay on the device, in local memory.

It has memory

It remembers what you're working on between conversations. Your holdings, your theses, your goals, your search pipeline. It picks up where you left off, not from scratch every time.

It has tools

Live market data, SEC filings, web search, Reddit, X, YouTube, podcasts. Curunir reaches for the right source on its own and cites it inline. Every claim traces back to a source you can open yourself.

It fact-checks itself

Every research output goes through a separate fact-checker agent before you see it. The agent that wrote the answer is not the agent that verified it. If the evidence doesn't support a claim, it gets corrected or cut.

You can reach it from anywhere

Web, CLI, or email, through a thin portal that passes the conversation along and keeps the data where it is. Ask from your phone and the answer comes from the box on your desk.

Why it's different

Every other AI assistant you've used lives in someone else's cloud. Your conversations, your questions, your financial data, your health worries sit in a database that belongs to a trillion-dollar company. Indexed, searchable, one policy change away from being used in ways you didn't agree to.

Curunir flips that. The intelligence is rented. The data is owned.

You get the agent without handing over the parts that are actually yours.

It's a box on your desk that thinks. It remembers. It cites its sources. And it keeps your private life private, not by promising to, but by design.
