// what it does

Built for serious research — and the memory it deserves.

01

Thorough on any topic.

Composable skills and sophisticated tools that pull from authoritative sources to investigate a question thoroughly, not summarize the first page of results.

02

Learns what works for you.

Learns from every interaction what works for you, what doesn't, and where it can help you do better. The more you use it, the less you have to explain.

03

Remembers every conversation.

Past research, dropped threads, half-formed hypotheses — all kept. Ask in August about something you said in March; it'll find it, and pick up where you left off.

// where it lives

All of this at home. Not in the cloud.

Curunir runs on a small device in your home or lab. The language models live in the cloud — but your data doesn't. Your research, your conversations, and your preferences stay on the device.

The assistant is still reachable from anywhere — web, CLI, email — through a thin portal that brokers the conversation without copying the data.

Under the hood, it draws on a mix of frontier models — routing each task to the one best suited to it, whether that's deep reasoning, fast synthesis, or vision. No single-vendor lock-in, no one-size-fits-all.
