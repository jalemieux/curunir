MEMO 02
CATALYST → STOCK
may 4–11, 2026
Is retatrutide a blockbuster or a bust for Eli Lilly (LLY)? Bull case, bear case, and what it does to the stock.

Retatrutide (LY3437943) is Lilly's triple agonist. It posted 28.7% average weight loss at 68 weeks in Phase 3 — the highest ever recorded, ahead of tirzepatide and semaglutide.

  • Bull: best-in-class efficacy; Street peak-sales estimates run $5–30B.
  • Bear: 18.2% adverse-event discontinuation, cannibalization of Zepbound/Mounjaro, and Novo Nordisk competition.
  • Honest caveat: the 28.7% comes from TRIUMPH-4, a bariatric/OA population — it may not generalize until the general-obesity trials report.

Stock impact: LLY trades around $963; approval could drive $1,200–1,300 (consensus ~$1,220), a setback −15–25%. Memo attached.

Verdict — Partly priced in; asymmetric on the readout
