# Senior Director, Agentic AI Platform — Adobe (Experience Cloud)

**Location:** San Jose, CA (hybrid, 3 days on-site)
**Reports to:** VP of AI Platform, Experience Cloud
**Hiring Manager:** Priya Raman (priya.raman@adobe.com)
**Team size:** 14 direct reports (3 EMs, 9 ICs, 2 TPMs) — growing to ~25 in FY27
**Comp band:** $320K–$380K base + equity + bonus

---

## About the role

Adobe Experience Cloud is standing up a first-party **Agentic AI Platform** to power autonomous marketing workflows across Marketo, Campaign, and Journey Optimizer. We're looking for a Senior Director to own the platform end-to-end: agent runtime, tool interfaces, evaluation infrastructure, and the developer experience for internal product teams building on top.

This is a platform role, not a product role. You will not ship customer-facing features. You will enable 6+ downstream product teams to ship AI features on a unified substrate.

---

## What you'll do

- Own the technical roadmap for the Agentic AI Platform (3-year horizon)
- Hire, grow, and manage a team of ~25 engineers across agent infra, tool ecosystem, and evals
- Define the internal platform API contract that downstream product teams build against
- Partner with Research to productize LLM routing, tool-use reliability, and agent evaluation
- Drive architecture reviews, capacity planning, and platform SLA conversations
- Represent the platform in executive forums (CTO staff, quarterly business reviews)
- Own platform budget (~$18M/yr cloud + model inference)

---

## What we're looking for

**Required:**
- 15+ years in engineering, 8+ in engineering management (multiple levels of reports)
- Track record shipping a developer platform at scale (internal or external)
- Deep familiarity with LLM application patterns: tool use, RAG, agent loops, evaluations
- Experience navigating large-matrix organizations (Adobe has 30K+ engineers)

**Nice to have:**

- Experience with multi-model / cost-optimized inference (OpenRouter, Bedrock, Vertex routing)
