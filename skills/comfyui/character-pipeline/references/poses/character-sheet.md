# Character sheet

Four angles around the subject in 60-degree increments, neutral pose
throughout. The front and back angles are already covered by the
locked **reference images** from Stage 2 of the character pipeline,
so this pack focuses on the in-between angles (three-quarter views
and straight profiles) on both left and right sides.

Use this when 2 sides isn't enough — comic / game character bibles,
LoRAs that need to render the subject from arbitrary angles, or when
the character has asymmetric features (one-sided tattoos, scars, hair
parting) that benefit from being seen from both left and right.

Doubles the Stage 3 wall time vs. `turnaround.md` — flag this to the
user before kicking off.

## Poses

### three-quarter-left
three-quarter view rotated toward camera-left, standing with weight evenly distributed, arms relaxed at sides, face in three-quarter view gazing toward the lens

### profile-left
full side profile facing camera-left, standing on a plain studio floor with weight evenly distributed, arms relaxed at sides, gaze level toward the left edge of frame

### profile-right
full side profile facing camera-right, standing on a plain studio floor with weight evenly distributed, arms relaxed at sides, gaze level toward the right edge of frame

### three-quarter-right
three-quarter view rotated toward camera-right, standing with weight evenly distributed, arms relaxed at sides, face in three-quarter view gazing toward the lens
