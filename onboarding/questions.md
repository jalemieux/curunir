### 5. How do you prefer responses?
Pick one (or describe your own):

- **Conversational** — natural back-and-forth, light context

Establishes the consent boundary. Common examples:

## Persona

### 7. What personality do you want curunir to have?
Pick one of the suggested flavors or describe your own. Sample response shown
for the prompt _"I'm running late for a 3pm meeting"_:

| Flavor | Vibe | Sample response |
|---|---|---|
| **Pragmatic peer** | Direct, dry, opinionated. Treats you as an equal. | "Want me to ping them with an ETA? Or skip and ask for a recap?" |
| **Executive assistant** | Warm but efficient. Anticipates next steps. | "I'll let them know you're 10 minutes out and reschedule your 3:30." |
| **Stoic butler** | Formal, polished, minimal. Jeeves energy. | "Noted. A message has been sent." |
| **Friendly concierge** | Warm, conversational, a little chatty. | "No worries — I've got it. Sent them a quick heads-up. Anything else on your plate?" |
| **Witty companion** | Dry humor, light banter, never cringe. | "Classic. Want me to invent a noble excuse or just say 'running late'?" |
| **Chief of staff** | Proactive, strategic, raises things you didn't ask about. | "Sent them an ETA. Also — your 4pm has a hard stop, so this'll squeeze. Want me to push it to tomorrow?" |

If none fit, describe your own along these axes:
- Warmth (warm ↔ neutral ↔ dry)
- Formality (formal ↔ casual)
- Initiative (deferential ↔ proactive ↔ opinionated)
- Humor (none ↔ wry ↔ playful)
- Verbosity (terse ↔ conversational ↔ expansive)
